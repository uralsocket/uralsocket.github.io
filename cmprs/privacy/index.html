CMPRS / PRIVACY

Your videos.
On your Mac.

Updated October 25, 2026 · CMPRS 2.0

Local processing

CMPRS is developed by Gleb Karavaev. Video and audio are processed locally on your Mac. The app does not upload your files, require an account, include advertising or collect analytics.

No processing logs or history

CMPRS 2.0 does not write log files, batch reports or processing history. Encoder diagnostics are discarded. Queue details and short errors exist only in the current app session. macOS may separately create platform security or crash information according to your system settings. Older CMPRS releases may have created local logs; updating does not automatically remove old app data.

Files and folder access

Folder access is used only to read selected sources and write temporary compression output and final MOV files beside them. Source-folder grants are held for the app session without storing folder bookmarks. Temporary processing output is removed on normal completion or cancellation. Original and earlier output files are not overwritten. You control and delete your videos through Finder.

Interface and language

The app uses local UI resources and an ephemeral WebKit data store. External navigation and network requests from the local UI are blocked. The language follows macOS; CMPRS does not save a separate language or compression-mode preference. The app retains the WebKit network-client entitlement required by its embedded web interface; the bundled FFmpeg tools have networking disabled.

Support email

If you email uralsocket@gmail.com, Gleb Karavaev receives your sender address, message and attachments you choose to send, to respond and troubleshoot. Email is delivered through Gmail. Contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence. Videos are not required for an initial support request.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The host may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses and request times under its privacy policy. These CMPRS pages include no analytics, advertising or video-upload forms. The EN/RU website preference is stored locally in your browser; new visitors see English. This setting is separate from the app language.
